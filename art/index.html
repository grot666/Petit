Little Artworks · 小艺术品

Five pieces that keep moving

Generative works you can regenerate, touch, and download. 五件会动、可以触碰、可以下载的生成艺术。

I

Wind Trees

风中的树

L-system trees that grow branch by branch and sway in the wind.

II

Turing Patterns

图灵斑纹

Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion: spots, stripes and coral that you can stir.

III

Stained Glass

彩色玻璃

Voronoi cells leaded like a church window. Tap to add a pane.

IV

Pocket Galaxy

口袋星系

A slowly turning spiral of thousands of stars. Drag to tilt.

V

Kaleidoscope

万花筒

Draw one stroke, get a mirrored mandala in shifting colour.

Plain canvas, no libraries, nothing tracked.
