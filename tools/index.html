Little Tools · 小工具

Five small useful things

They run entirely in your browser. No accounts, no tracking. 五个小而有用的工具，全部在浏览器本地运行。

I

Pomodoro

番茄钟

25 minutes of focus, 5 of rest, with a soft chime and a tomato count.

II

Palette

配色生成

Five harmonious colours. Lock, shuffle, copy hex codes, share the link.

III

Decision Wheel

决定转盘

Write your options, spin, let the wheel decide what is for lunch.

IV

Word Counter

字数统计

Chinese characters and English words counted separately, with reading time.

V

Breathe

呼吸练习

Box, 4-7-8 or calm breathing, paced by a growing and shrinking circle.

Plain HTML & JavaScript. Settings stay in your browser.
